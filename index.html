Map the citation network of any researcher

Search for an author to visualise who cites their work, what they reference, and where the connections overlap. Data comes from OpenAlex — free and open, no account needed.

OpenAlex API key (optional)

Without a key, everyone on your network shares a free allowance of about 1,000 requests a day, which a large author can use up. A free key from openalex.org/settings/api gives you your own allowance of about 10,000 a day. The key is stored only in this browser.
